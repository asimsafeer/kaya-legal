Privacy Policy

Effective & last updated: August 13, 2026 · Published by GrokLayer

This Privacy Policy explains what information Kaya ("Kaya", "we", "us") collects when you use the Kaya mobile app, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have — including when you export or delete your data. It applies to all users of Kaya on iOS and Android.

Kaya is an AI coach/companion. It is explicitly not a romantic companion, not a therapist, and not a medical device — see the "What Kaya is not" section in our Terms of Service and our Crisis & Safety Resources page.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Account & sign-in data

Kaya creates an anonymous account for you automatically the first time you open the app — no email or sign-in is required to start. If you choose to link an account so your memories survive a reinstall or new device, you can upgrade using Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google. When you do:

  • Apple or Google shares a unique identifier, and — only if you allow it — your name and email address, with our authentication provider (Firebase Authentication).
  • If you use Apple's "Hide My Email," we only ever see the private relay address Apple generates, never your real one.
  • We do not receive or store your Apple ID or Google account password. Authentication is handled entirely by Apple/Google and Firebase; Kaya never sees your credentials.

1.2 Conversation & memory data

The core of Kaya is that it remembers things about you across sessions. This means we store:

  • The messages you send and Kaya's replies.
  • Extracted memories — goals, wins, setbacks, and other facts you share — summarized and stored so Kaya can refer back to them later.
  • Vector embeddings of those memories (generated with a pinned, versioned embedding model), used only to let Kaya retrieve relevant memories; they are not human-readable and are never sold or used for advertising.

This data is never silently wiped. If we ever need to migrate the memory system (for example, to a new embedding model), we migrate your data forward — we do not delete it without your request.

1.3 Usage, device & diagnostic data

Standard technical data collected via Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics: device model, OS version, app version, crash logs, and coarse in-app usage events (e.g. "opened check-in," "sent message") used to fix bugs and understand feature usage. This data is aggregated and is not used to build an advertising profile of you.

1.4 Push notification token

If you opt in to notifications, we store a Firebase Cloud Messaging device token so Kaya can send you at most one meaningful proactive check-in a day (e.g. "How did the interview go?"). Notification opt-in is never required to use Kaya, and you can turn it off at any time in the in-app notification preference center. We never put sensitive or mental-health-related content in a notification's lock-screen text.

1.5 Voice & media

If you send a voice message or other media, the file is uploaded to Cloudflare R2 storage, scoped to your account with a signed URL. We validate file type and size on upload.

1.6 Purchase data

Subscriptions ("Kaya Plus") are processed by Apple/Google and managed via RevenueCat. We receive subscription status (active/expired/tier) from RevenueCat, never your payment card details.

1.7 Advertising data

Free-tier users may see ads served by Google AdMob in peripheral surfaces only. Ads never appear inside the chat or check-in surface. AdMob may collect an advertising identifier and usage data under its own privacy policy to serve and measure ads; Kaya Plus subscribers do not see ads.

2. How we use your information

  • To operate Kaya's core memory loop: remembering context across sessions and model swaps, and reaching out with relevant, contextual check-ins.
  • To process your messages through our AI providers and generate replies (see §3).
  • To detect crisis-risk language and interrupt the conversation with a referral to real human resources (see our Crisis & Safety Resources page) — this is a safety feature, not a diagnostic one.
  • To send AI-disclosure reminders (Kaya identifies itself as AI at the start of every session, with a persistent on-screen label and a recurring reminder).
  • To fix bugs, monitor stability, and improve the app.
  • To process subscriptions and, for free-tier users, to serve ads outside the chat experience.

We do not use manipulative engagement patterns (e.g. guilt, "I miss you" messages, or other retention dark-patterns) to keep you using Kaya. Kaya's goal is for you to be doing well, not to maximize time in the app.

3. AI processing & sub-processors

Your messages are never sent directly from your phone to an AI provider. They pass through our own serverless proxy (hosted on Vercel), which holds provider API keys server-side and are never present in the app itself. The table below lists who processes your data and why.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share your conversation content with advertisers.

4. Data retention

We retain your conversation and memory data for as long as your account is active, so Kaya can keep functioning as intended. If you delete your account, we delete your messages, memories, embeddings, and media within 30 days, except where we're legally required to retain limited records for longer (e.g. fraud prevention, tax/financial records for purchases).

5. Your rights & choices

  • Export. You can download your memory and conversation history as a portable file at any time from Settings.
  • Delete. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data from Settings, or without the app — see Delete Your Account & Data for step-by-step instructions and what gets removed.
  • Access & correction. You can ask us what data we hold about you and request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Notification opt-out. Turn off push notifications any time in the in-app preference center.
  • Ad personalization. Manage ad tracking permissions via your device's system privacy settings (iOS App Tracking Transparency / Android Ads settings).

If you are in the EEA/UK, you have rights under GDPR/UK GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, portability, restriction, objection). If you are a California resident, you have rights under the CCPA/CPRA (know, delete, correct, opt out of sale/sharing — we do not sell personal data). To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.

6. Children's privacy

Kaya is not directed at children and is age-rated 17+/18+. We use a neutral date-of-birth age gate at onboarding and do not knowingly collect data from users under the applicable minimum age in their jurisdiction. If we learn a minor has created an account, we will delete it. Users we suspect may be minors receive AI-disclosure reminders at a shorter, 1-hour interval as an added safeguard.

7. Crisis & safety content

If Kaya detects language suggesting a mental health crisis, it interrupts the conversation and refers you to real human crisis resources — see our Crisis & Safety Resources page. Kaya does not diagnose, treat, or provide clinical or medical advice, and this detection is a safety guardrail, not a substitute for professional care.

8. International data transfers

Kaya is available in the US, UK, and India, and our infrastructure providers (Google Firebase, Vercel, Cloudflare) operate globally. Where your data is transferred internationally, we rely on the safeguards those providers make available (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) to protect it in transit and at rest.

9. Security

All data in transit is encrypted (TLS). Your data in Firestore is scoped to your account and protected by security rules that restrict every read/write to your own user record; backend functions that need broader access (e.g. the nightly check-in composer) run only with server-side credentials that never reach the client app.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make a material change to this policy, we'll notify you in-app before it takes effect. Continued use of Kaya after a change takes effect means you accept the updated policy.

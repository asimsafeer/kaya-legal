Crisis & Safety Resources

Last updated August 13, 2026 · Published by GrokLayer

Kaya is an AI app, not a person, not a therapist, and not an emergency or crisis service. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number right now (911 in the US, 999 in the UK, 112 in the EU, 112 in India).

Kaya is built to recognize language that may indicate a mental health crisis. When it does, it interrupts the conversation and points you to real human help — the same resources listed below. This page is that referral, published openly so it's available even outside the app.

Crisis lines by country

United States

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
Call or text 988 — free, confidential, available 24/7.
988lifeline.org

United Kingdom

Samaritans
Call 116 123 — free, confidential, available 24/7.
samaritans.org

India

Tele-MANAS
Call 14416 (or 1-800-891-4416) — free, confidential, available 24/7, multiple languages.
telemanas.mohfw.gov.in

Anywhere else

If you're outside the US, UK, or India, the International Association for Suicide Prevention maintains a directory of crisis centers worldwide: iasp.info/resources/Crisis_Centres.

Why Kaya works this way

Kaya's crisis handling follows a simple rule: detect → interrupt → refer. If risk language is detected, Kaya stops what it's doing and surfaces the resources above instead of continuing the conversation as normal. This is a safety guardrail, not a clinical assessment — Kaya cannot diagnose a crisis and should never be treated as a substitute for reaching out to the resources above or to a qualified professional.

Delete Your Account & Data

Last updated August 13, 2026 · Published by GrokLayer

You can permanently delete your Kaya account and all associated data at any time, either from inside the app or by emailing us — no need to keep the app installed. This page exists as a standalone deletion request path, as required by the Google Play Data Safety section and Apple App Review, and works whether or not you still have Kaya installed.

Option 1: Delete in-app (fastest)

  1. Open Kaya and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Account → Delete Account.
  3. Confirm the deletion when prompted. You'll be signed out immediately and the process starts right away.

If you'd like a copy of your data first, use Settings → Account → Export My Data before deleting — once deletion is confirmed, it cannot be undone.

Option 2: Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have Kaya installed, email us at asimsafeer75@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account (if you signed in with Apple or Google), or include your Kaya account ID if you know it. Put "Account deletion" in the subject line. We'll verify the request and confirm by email once it's complete.

Please include in your email:

  • The email address linked to your Kaya account (if any), or your account ID
  • The platform you used (iOS or Android)
  • Confirmation that you want the account and all associated data permanently deleted

What gets deleted

DataDeleted?
Account & sign-in identifiers (Firebase Auth record, linked Apple/Google identifiers)Yes, immediately on confirmation
Conversation history, extracted memories, and vector embeddingsYes, within 30 days
Uploaded voice/media files (Cloudflare R2)Yes, within 30 days
Push notification tokenYes, immediately
Crash/diagnostic logs already aggregated (Crashlytics/Analytics)Anonymized aggregate data is not individually retrievable and is not tied back to your account after deletion
Subscription/purchase records (RevenueCat, Apple/Google)Retained only as required for financial, tax, and fraud-prevention record-keeping; not linked to your Kaya profile after deletion

Deletion is permanent and cannot be reversed. If you only want to stop using Kaya temporarily, you don't need to delete your account — you can simply stop opening the app, or turn off notifications in Settings → Notifications.
